# B2B Wedge Expansion Example

## Initial wedge
A small workflow automation startup launched with one narrow capability: triaging inbound support tickets for SaaS companies. The first version only handled tier-one repetitive issues and escalated uncertain cases to humans.

## Expansion path
After proving response time improvements, the team expanded to adjacent workflows such as renewal risk detection and account health summarization. This expansion was possible because the original wedge created trust and data access.

## Lesson
Credible expansion depends on solving a painful entry point first. Teams that launch broad suites too early often fail to reach depth in any single workflow.
