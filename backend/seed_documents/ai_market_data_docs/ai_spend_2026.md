# AI Software Market Notes (2026)

## Enterprise demand signal
Across mid-market and enterprise accounts, buyers increasingly prioritize AI features that reduce operational cycle time. Products tied to measurable labor savings show faster procurement movement than generic assistant tools.

Security review remains a bottleneck. Teams that provide data residency controls, audit trails, and role-based access see better conversion in regulated sectors.

## Distribution trend
Horizontal AI tooling is crowded, but vertical offerings aligned to a specific role or workflow often achieve higher willingness to pay. Buyers reward domain context, prebuilt integrations, and credible implementation guidance.
